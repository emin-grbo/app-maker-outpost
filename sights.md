# sights — scene prompts for each track

One image prompt per song. Style matched to the reference: photoreal, lived-in interior in the foreground, huge windows framing a landscape, warm believable clutter, cinematic wide shot. Each entry ends with **Animate:** notes — the 2-4 subtle loops that make the still feel alive on YouTube.

---

## bunkerfog.strudel — "bunker fog"

Wide interior shot of a semi-underground coastal listening post at grey dawn. Concrete walls softened by decades of use: peeling maps of shipping lanes, tide charts, polaroids taped in rows, a wall of analog radio receivers with amber dials. A narrow horizontal viewport window runs along one wall, fog rolling over dark water and the silhouette of a rusted antenna mast outside. On the desk: a reel-to-reel recorder, headphones on a hook, enamel mug steaming, logbook open with pencil. Cold blue-grey light from the window mixing with warm tungsten desk lamp. Photorealistic, cinematic composition, shallow depth in corners, 4K ambience-channel aesthetic.

**Animate:** fog drifting past viewport; radio dial lights flickering irregularly; steam from mug; tape reels turning slowly.

## citadelfrost.strudel — "citadel frost"

Interior of a research outpost frozen into an arctic mountainside, deep polar night. Floor-to-ceiling triple-glazed windows, frost creeping in from the corners of the glass, looking out on a blue-black glacier valley under faint green aurora. Inside: banks of muted monitoring equipment with slow-blinking status LEDs, thick cables along the ceiling, a fur-lined parka on a hook, bunk with wool blankets in shadow. Single work desk lit by one cold white lamp, thermos and clipboard. Everything still, air visibly cold, breath-fog territory. Deep blue palette with one warm point of light. Photorealistic, cinematic, 4K sci-fi ambience aesthetic.

**Animate:** aurora slowly shifting; LEDs blinking out of sync; loose snow streaming past window; frost edge on glass almost imperceptibly growing.
